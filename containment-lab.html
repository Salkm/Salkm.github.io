Antivirus / SIEM / AI automation

An alert from an antivirus solution or SIEM starts the AI automation workflow. It gathers file, process and device evidence, uses AI to summarize the risk, then routes the case to a policy-authorized response or analyst review. Confirmed malware is quarantined; active ransomware takes an emergency isolation path when pre-authorized. Benign alerts are closed after review, while uncertain cases remain under investigation. Response results are verified and recorded.
